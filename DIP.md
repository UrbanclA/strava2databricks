Project ''strava2databricks'' is build #1 of my Data Integration Portfolio.

## Personal growth
What started as a desire to grow my data engineering and analytics expertise has quickly evolved into a personal challenge. 
 
Like any professional, I do my fair share of data wrangling at work, but cannot exactly share specifics publicly 
other than vaguely writing project names on a CV. It’s even worse for those small-scale pipelines that make the job easier. 
That one API call that lists every source table being replicated to the warehouse, complemented with a simple view that maps them by business domain? 
Try explaining the beauty of that automated joy-nugget to anyone outside your immediate team!

## The aim
Building a portfolio, a vault of knowledge, seems like the perfect way to document, explore, scale up, track one’s professional growth, and showcase direct impact on dataflow solutions.

The goal is one project or Data Integration Build (DIB) per year while following my interests, which currently revolve around sport-tracker data, smart home implementations and general code automation.
